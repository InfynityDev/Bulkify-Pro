# Bulkify Pro by Infynity.Dev

Transform your WooCommerce products into powerful wholesale contact pages with WhatsApp integration, custom buttons, and tiered bulk discount tables — perfect for B2B stores, wholesale businesses, and service providers.

---

## 🧩 Overview

Bulkify Pro is a professional WordPress/WooCommerce plugin that transforms selected WooCommerce products into fully interactive wholesale or service-order pages. Instead of traditional e-commerce checkout, the plugin replaces standard "Add to Cart" functionality with customizable WhatsApp contact buttons, custom contact buttons, itemized pricing tables, quantity controls, and bulk discount tables.

The plugin is ideal for wholesale businesses that require custom pricing based on quantity, B2B stores that prefer direct contact over automated checkout, service providers offering customized quotes, and multi-vendor platforms where each vendor needs unique contact methods. Bulkify Pro seamlessly integrates with your existing WooCommerce store, providing extensive customization options while maintaining performance and compatibility with modern WordPress and WooCommerce standards.

---

## ✨ Key Features

- **WhatsApp Integration** – Direct WhatsApp contact buttons with customizable message templates that include product details automatically
- **Custom Contact Buttons** – Create custom buttons linking to contact forms, phone calls, or any URL with full styling control
- **Bulk Discount Tables** – Display tiered pricing tables with quantity breaks, savings calculations, and "Best Value" badges
- **Custom Pricing Display** – Override WooCommerce prices with custom text, starting prices, and unit labels (e.g., "per item", "per kg")
- **Multi-language Support** – WPML and Polylang compatible with automatic RTL language support (Arabic, Hebrew, Farsi, Urdu)
- **Extensive Customization** – 11 pre-defined color schemes plus custom color options, font size controls, and appearance settings
- **Product-Level Control** – Per-product settings that override global defaults for maximum flexibility
- **Shortcodes** – Embed WhatsApp buttons, custom buttons, and bulk discounts anywhere using simple shortcodes
- **Performance Optimized** – Built-in caching system and optimized asset loading for fast page loads
- **HPOS Compatible** – Fully compatible with WooCommerce High-Performance Order Storage

## 🚀 Quick Start

1. **Upload the Plugin** – Navigate to `Plugins → Add New → Upload Plugin` in your WordPress dashboard and upload the Bulkify Pro ZIP file
2. **Activate** – Click `Install Now` and then `Activate` the plugin
3. **Configure Settings** – Go to `WooCommerce → Bulkify Pro` and enter your default WhatsApp number in international format (e.g., `905551234567`)
4. **Enable on a Product** – Edit any WooCommerce product, scroll to the `Bulkify Pro` meta box, enable WhatsApp button or bulk discounts, and save
5. **View Result** – Visit the product page on the frontend to see the "Add to Cart" button replaced with your configured contact buttons and bulk discount tables
